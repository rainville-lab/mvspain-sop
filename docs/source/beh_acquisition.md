## During the session

Welcome to the lab
- Offer bathroom and water 
- Welcome to the room and greet with a confident and grateful smile, thanking the participant for their time and interest in science


### Introduction to the study and FIC (~ 10 min.)

Introduce the study 

> Instructions :speech_balloon: 
This is a study about hypnosis. We are interested in understanding how you respond to it. Since hypnosis has been proven to be efficient for pain reduction, we use it in the context of experimental pain. You will be introduced to hypnosis in a prerecorded session, and in 2 subsequent visit, you will undergo hypnosis and pain experiment, in two different brain recording settings. 


- Sign both behavioral and MRI-EEG consent forms (FIC)


Go over the MRI counter-indications with the participant
- if there are evident risks of not completing the MRI session, we can thank the person for their time
- No payment in this case

- Mention explicitly that MRI requires to be in a tunnel...
- We can organize a mock scan in case the person feels stressed about it
